# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project harassment-free and respectful for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and evidence-based.
- Welcome good-faith questions and corrections.
- Protect other people's private decision records and personal information.
- Focus criticism on ideas, behavior, and artifacts rather than people.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, sharing private data without permission, sustained disruption, or bad-faith personal attacks are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns through the repository's private reporting channel. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when this code is violated. Reports will be handled as privately as practical.
